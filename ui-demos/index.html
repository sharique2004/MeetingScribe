MeetingScribe · UI exploration

Four takes on a calmer transcript

The current transcript screen stacks four control bands before a single line of dialogue appears, so nothing leads the eye. These four directions each fix that a different way — by subtraction, by atmosphere, by motion, or by structure. Click into any one and try it: switch tabs, search, resize the text, click a timestamp, hit play.

Every demo renders the same dummy meeting (a “Q3 Roadmap Sync” with three speakers) so you’re comparing design, not content. Nothing here is wired to the real app — these live in ui-demos/ and change nothing in MeetingScribe.

Premium and a little magical, but calm — slow ambient light and glass carry the richness instead of controls.

Split Focus

two-pane workspace

Separate reading from acting. The transcript stays bare; everything else moves to a context rail plus a ⌘K palette.

  • Clean left transcript, swapping right rail (Summary / Speakers / Ask)
  • ⌘K command palette is the one place to do anything
  • Speaker minimap seam shows who talks where
